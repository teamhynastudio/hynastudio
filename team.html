Hyna Studio Technologies LLP is an Indian technology and product innovation company building digital products and platforms designed to help businesses manage, connect, grow, and operate more efficiently.

Our Mission: Hyna Studio is working towards building an integrated business ecosystem that helps entrepreneurs, startups, small businesses, and organizations access the tools, connections, services, and opportunities they need to grow — one unified ecosystem for managing your business and connecting with the people and opportunities that help it grow.

HYNA DNA: HYNA DNA is a business-focused platform designed to help business owners manage and grow their businesses. Depending on the product version and available features, HYNA DNA may provide tools for business management, customer relationship management (CRM), customer and business connections, quotation management, sales tracking, products and services management, business analytics, AI-powered assistance, growth opportunities, and professional connections. Features and services may change as the platform continues to develop.
